# Quant Foundations
## Overview

This repository documents my structured mathematical and computational preparation for a career in:

- Quantitative Development
- Quantitative Research
- Computational Finance

I do not currently hold a full mathematics degree.

Rather than waiting for ideal circumstances, I am rebuilding mathematical foundations deliberately and in public — progressing from GCSE/A-Level equivalence through undergraduate-level mathematics and into quantitative finance.

This repository serves as:

- A structured academic roadmap
- A portfolio of applied quantitative projects
- A public study log
- Evidence of long-term commitment to mathematical rigour
---
# Roadmap

The pathway is divided into four structured stages:

- **Stage 0 – Mathematical Foundations (GCSE → A-Level equivalent)**
- **Stage 1 – Core Undergraduate Mathematics**
- **Stage 2 – Mathematical Maturity & Applied Methods**
- **Stage 3 – Quantitative Finance & Computational Specialisation**

Each stage includes:

- Formal coursework (Coursera-based structured learning)
- Applied Python implementations
- Mathematical derivations
- Project work
